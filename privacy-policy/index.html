CSV to PDF Converter Pro

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

CSV to PDF Converter Pro (“the app”) converts CSV files into PDF documents. This policy describes what information is used when you use the app.

CSV files and PDFs

CSV files are opened and processed on your device. The app does not upload the contents of your CSV files or generated PDFs to the app developer or to RevenueCat. When you choose to save or share a PDF, Apple’s system interface passes the file to the destination you select. That destination may handle the file under its own privacy policy.

The app may create temporary PDF files on your device to support sharing. App preferences, including saved PDF styles and whether the first free export has been used, are stored locally using iOS app storage. The app does not provide account registration or cloud synchronization for these files or preferences.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes App Store purchases and subscriptions. The app uses RevenueCat to retrieve available purchase offerings, verify purchase status, restore purchases, and provide Pro features.

The RevenueCat SDK is configured without a user-provided account ID, so it generates an anonymous app user identifier. To provide purchase and entitlement services, RevenueCat may process purchase or transaction information, the anonymous identifier, and limited technical information such as device type and operating system. CSV contents and generated PDFs are not sent to RevenueCat. Apple and RevenueCat handle purchase data under their own policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

The app does not use purchase information for cross-app advertising or tracking.

Information sent when you contact support

If you email support, the developer receives the email address, message, and any files or other details you choose to include. Do not email sensitive CSV or PDF files unless they are needed to resolve your request.

Data retention and choices

App preferences remain on the device until you change them or remove the app. Temporary files are managed by the app and iOS. Apple and RevenueCat may retain purchase records as described in their policies and as required to provide purchase services or comply with law.

You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account subscription settings. To ask a privacy question or request deletion of support correspondence, contact akankobatengr@gmail.com.

Children

The app is a general-purpose file utility and does not require an account. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. Purchases are processed by Apple under its own age and family controls.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or service providers change. The effective date above will be updated when the policy changes.

Contact

For questions about this policy, email akankobatengr@gmail.com.
